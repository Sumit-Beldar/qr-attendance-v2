Attendance Help & Troubleshooting

How to Mark Attendance

  1. Log in to your student account with your Student ID and password.
  2. Tap Scan QR Code to open your phone camera.
  3. Point your camera at the rotating QR code shown on the classroom screen.
  4. If your camera is unavailable or permission is blocked, tap Enter code manually and type the 6-character code below the QR.

Camera Permission Help

Android (Chrome): Tap the lock or tune icon in the address bar → Site settings → Camera → Allow.

iPhone (Safari): Tap the aA icon in the address bar → Website Settings → Camera → Allow.

After allowing permissions, refresh the page and tap Scan QR Code again.

When running on a local network without an internet domain, your browser will show a standard certificate notice the first time you connect.

Google Chrome (Android)

  1. On the "Your connection is not private" screen, tap Advanced at the bottom.
  2. Tap Proceed to ... (unsafe).
  3. You will be taken directly to the attendance login page.

Safari (iPhone / iPad)

  1. On the "This Connection Is Not Private" page, tap Show Details.
  2. Scroll down and tap visit this website.
  3. Confirm by tapping Visit Website in the popup dialog.

Network Troubleshooting

Phones and laptop must be on the same network.

College Wi-Fi often blocks device-to-device traffic. If phones cannot connect, turn on your phone's mobile hotspot and connect both the teacher's laptop and the students to it.

On Windows, ensure Node.js is allowed through the Windows Defender Firewall on Private networks.

If your phone keeps dropping the local connection, turn off cellular mobile data temporarily so it stays on the local Wi-Fi.
